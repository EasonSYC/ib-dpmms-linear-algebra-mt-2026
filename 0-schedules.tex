\section*{Schedules}
\label{sec:schedules}

\contentSection{3}{Definition of a vector space (over \(\RR\) or \(\CC\)), subspaces, the space spanned by a subset. Linear independence, bases, dimension. Direct sums and complementary subspaces. Quotient spaces.}

\contentSection{4}{Linear maps, isomorphisms. Relation between rank and nullity. The space of linear maps from \(U\) to \(V\), representation by matrices. Change of basis. Row rank and column rank.}

\contentSection{3}{Determinant and trace of a square matrix. Determinant of a product of two matrices and of the inverse matrix. Determinant of an endomorphism. The adjugate matrix.}

\contentSection{4}{Eigenvalues and eigenvectors. Diagonal and triangular forms. Characteristic and minimal polynomials. Cayley--Hamilton Theorem over \(\CC\). Algebraic and geometric multiplicity of eigenvalues. Statement and illustration of Jordan normal form.}

\contentSection{2}{Dual of a finite-dimensional vector space, dual bases and maps. Matrix representation, rank and determinant of dual map}

\contentSection{4}{Bilinear forms. Matrix representation, change of basis. Symmetric forms and their link with quadratic forms. Diagonalisation of quadratic forms. Law of inertia, classification by rank and signature. Complex Hermitian forms.}

\contentSection{4}{Inner product spaces, orthonormal sets, orthogonal projection, \(V = W \oplus W^\perp\). Gram--Schmidt orthogonalisation. Adjoints. Diagonalisation of Hermitian matrices. Orthogonality of eigenvectors and properties of eigenvalues.}
